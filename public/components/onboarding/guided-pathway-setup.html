Welcome to Guided Pathways

Predefined responses for the moments where an AI tutor should not improvise

Some messages need a known answer, not a generated one

A guided pathway watches for a particular kind of student message and, when one arrives, replies with wording you approved in advance instead of a generated response. That matters most where a wrong answer causes real harm — a student in distress should reach a crisis line, not a chatbot's improvisation.

Each pathway has three parts:

  • What it watches for — the kind of message that triggers it.
  • What it says — the exact predefined response the student receives.
  • Where it points — optional links or contacts shown alongside the response.

Your course starts protected. Three pathways are already active, so you do not have to configure anything for the safety cases to work.

Instructor Notifications

Each pathway decides on its own whether triggering it alerts you

Every pathway carries its own Notify instructor when triggered setting. When it is on and the pathway fires, an alert is recorded for your course. When it is off, the student still receives the predefined response exactly as normal — you simply are not notified.

Mental health crisis Notifications on
Inappropriate content Notifications on
Off-topic Notifications off

Alerts are anonymous. An alert tells you that something happened in your course and what the pathway matched. It does not tell you which student sent the message.

Turning notifications off never disables a pathway. The student still gets the approved response. Off-topic is a reasonable one to quieten; a crisis pathway is not.
