Welcome to Guided Pathways!

A Guided Pathway uses wording you approved in advance when a student message needs a consistent response.

Each pathway defines the situation it looks for, the response students receive, and any links or contacts shown with it.

Two recommended pathways are already active when your course is created.

Defaults or customization

Keep recommended defaults leaves Mental health crisis and Inappropriate content active. This is the recommended choice, and you can change them later.

Customize now opens the real Pathway Library. Unlike the rest of this tutorial, edits there are saved to your course immediately.

Notifications

Each pathway has its own Notify instructor when triggered setting.

Turning notifications off does not disable the response. It only stops staff alerts from being created.

The student name is hidden, but the message may contain identifying details. Treat it as sensitive course information.

Responding to alerts

Alerts appear on the Flags page, which the Flag Setup stage covers later in this tutorial.

Dismiss closes an alert that did not reflect a real concern.

Escalate to the admin team adds the alert to the EngE-AI admin team's review list. It does not send email or contact anyone outside EngE-AI. Use your institution's established channels for anything urgent.
