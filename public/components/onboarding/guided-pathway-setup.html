Welcome to Guided Pathways

Predefined responses for the moments where an AI tutor should not improvise

Some messages need a known answer

A guided pathway watches for a defined kind of student message and replies with approved wording instead of a generated response. This is especially important when an improvised answer could cause harm.

Each pathway has three parts:

  • What it watches for — the kind of message that triggers it.
  • What it says — the exact predefined response the student receives.
  • Where it points — optional links or contacts shown alongside the response.

Your course starts protected. Three pathways are already active, so you do not have to configure anything for the safety cases to work.

Pathway Notifications

Each pathway controls whether it records an alert for course staff

Every pathway carries the production setting Notify instructor when triggered. When it is on, a trigger records an alert for course staff. When it is off, the student still receives the same predefined response, but no course alert is recorded.

Mental health crisis Notifications on
Inappropriate content Notifications on
Off-topic Notifications off

The student field is anonymous. Treat the message as sensitive because a student may include identifying information in their own text.

Notifications do not control the response. Turning them off leaves the approved student response active.
